## 1. Locked hypotheses (4 Oct 2026)

| | Prediction | Measured by | Proven wrong if |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | More than half of width-3 seeds learn the same lines, or nearly the same | Share of converged pairs whose matched lines are all within the angle and offset thresholds | Half or fewer of the pairs pass |
| H1b | Seeds with higher training error have lines further from the others | Correlation between each seed's training error and its median line distance to all other seeds, excluded seeds included | Correlation is zero or negative |
| H2 | The aligned blend is slightly worse, about 1.8 points | Median aligned (`wm`) error barrier at width 3 | Median falls outside the "slightly" band (0.5 to 3 points, dated 8 Oct) |
| H3 | The barrier gets smaller as width grows | Median aligned barrier across widths 3 to 64 | Width 64's barrier is not smaller than width 3's; rise-then-fall counts as a partial miss |

Clarifications: "1.8" means 1.8 percentage points of error. H2 refers to aligned networks, `wm` mode.

## 3. Learning-rate stability check (rule written 7 Oct, before the check)

Five seeds at widths 3 and 64, same data and settings as the main run, training loss only (no barriers, no alignments, no error). Keep lr = 0.01 if, for every one of the 10 runs:
1. the loss is always finite;
2. after step 200, the loss never rises more than 10% above the lowest loss so far;
3. the final loss is within 5% of the run's lowest loss.

Outcome: 10 of 10 passed (7 Oct), so lr = 0.01 was kept.
